About the author

I got tired of watching skilled people treat their abilities like random facts about themselves.

A person can know how to lead, build, troubleshoot, communicate, organize, create and solve problems and still have no idea how those abilities fit together.

That's what Hella Skillz is about.

Not convincing you that you're talented. Helping you understand what to do with the talent.

Because being skilled without structure creates motion, not momentum.
